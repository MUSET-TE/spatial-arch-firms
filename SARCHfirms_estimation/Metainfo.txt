
Name of Quantlet: 'SARCHfirms_estimation'

Published in: 'Spatial Effects in Local Productivity Volatility: A Non-Dynamic Spatial ARCH Analysis of Romanian Localities'

Description: 'Main estimation of the paper. Fits the non-dynamic spatial ARCH and SARspARCH models of Otto, Schmid and Garthoff (2018) to the two way fixed effect residuals of locality labour productivity, year by year over the 16 annual cross sections, and also to the unfiltered variable so that the spatial autoregressive term in the mean can be seen before filtering. Includes the log form of the variance equation and its AIC comparison against the linear form. Produces Table 1 of the paper.'

Keywords: 'spatial ARCH, spARCH, SARspARCH, conditional heteroskedasticity, quasi maximum likelihood, fixed effects, log spARCH, AIC, Romania'

Author: 'Codruta Mare, Mihai-Adrian Achim, Gabriela-Mihaela Muresan'

Submitted: '23. Nov 2026, Mihai-Adrian Achim'

Input: 'panel_uat.rds from SARCHfirms_data'

Output: 'sparch_fe_p50_k5.csv, sparch_prod_p50_k5.csv, logsparch_fe.csv, tabel_master_articol.csv'
