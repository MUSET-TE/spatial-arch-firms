
Name of Quantlet: 'SARCHfirms_inference'

Published in: 'Spatial Effects in Local Productivity Volatility: A Non-Dynamic Spatial ARCH Analysis of Romanian Localities'

Description: 'Monte Carlo study of the estimator on exactly the lattice used in the paper, not on a smaller convenient one. Data are simulated with a known spatial ARCH parameter, under normal errors and under the empirical distribution of the real residuals, and the parameter recovered by quasi maximum likelihood is recorded. Rejection at a nominal 5 per cent reaches 18 per cent under the empirical distribution against 2 per cent under normality, which is why inference in the paper rests on Moran I applied to the squared filtered shocks rather than on the analytic standard errors.'

Keywords: 'Monte Carlo, size and power, quasi maximum likelihood, empirical distribution, over rejection, distribution free inference, spatial ARCH, Romania'
